What’s on your plate?

Test a food name, a product barcode, or a photo. Recognized foods use your local model. Unrecognized photos or names are sent to Google Gemini for closest matches when configured. Barcodes use Barcode Lookup, with nutrition from Open Food Facts when available. Explore likely recipe ingredients, confirm their weights, and build an ingredient nutrition profile.


Checking local model…

Standard Nutri-Score is A–E. Local foods use a separate experimental A–F heuristic. Values are recipe matches, not measurements of your meal. Ingredients are inferred from a suggested recipe; the photo model does not segment individual ingredients.
